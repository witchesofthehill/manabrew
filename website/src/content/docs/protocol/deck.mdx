The whole deck - its identity, the maindeck and sideboard, the supplementary
zones, and the preserved client-side metadata.

<ProtocolType type="Deck" />

## DeckCard

One entry in a deck: a nested `identity` (which card and printing) plus a
flattened rules summary and the UI-only `uris`.

<ProtocolType type="DeckCard" />

## DeckCardIdentity

Which card a `DeckCard` is - name, printing (`setCode`/`cardNumber`), and an
optional `foil` flag. `id` is a client-side instance id.

<ProtocolType type="DeckCardIdentity" />

## DeckLabel

A user-defined label attached to a deck.
