## Prompt set

Shared direction: one original fantasy card game token illustration; painterly premium fantasy trading card artwork, tactile rich details, warm charcoal and antique gold palette, appetizing food when applicable. Portrait 2:3 composition, central subject legible in a square crop. Edge-to-edge artwork only: no text, letters, card border, logo, or collage.

- food-feast: Mouthwatering roast chicken with crisp golden skin, buttered potatoes and fresh herbs on a pewter platter, warm medieval tavern candlelight.
- food-pie: Mouthwatering rustic berry pie, flaky golden pastry broken open revealing glossy blackberries, honey and cream, enchanted woodland bakery.
- food-bread: Mouthwatering freshly torn sourdough bread, melting butter, ripe figs and artisan cheese on a wooden board in a fantasy inn.
- clue-key: An ornate antique brass key on a cryptic parchment map beside a magnifying glass, mysterious candlelit investigator desk.
- clue-locket: An opened silver locket beside a wax sealed letter and raven feather, moonlight and amber candlelight, fantasy mystery.
- clue-tracks: A luminous blue footprint in damp soil beside a torn embroidered ribbon, enchanted forest mystery, golden lantern illumination.
- treasure-coins: An overflowing small ancient treasure chest of gleaming gold coins and rubies, warm amber highlights in a fantasy vault.
- treasure-crown: A richly detailed golden crown with emeralds resting on burgundy velvet and scattered ancient coins, fantasy royal treasury.
- treasure-relic: A luminous sapphire in an ornate golden reliquary surrounded by pearl strands and coins in an ancient fantasy ruin.

Artwork is selected once per page session and controller/token kind; identical tokens share the same variant. Named cards and unsupported token kinds retain their existing image source. New token kinds can be added to tokenArtwork.ts with matching assets.
